Design Notes

  1. Stair kept in place. The stair hall sits directly above the ground floor's existing staircase, so the flight can simply continue upward without moving any load-bearing wall.
  2. Perimeter walls align. Every exterior wall of this plan traces the ground floor's 1280 × 898 cm outline, so the first floor loads transfer straight down to the ground floor walls and footing.
  3. Balcony replaces porch/sit-out. The wide front balcony sits over the ground floor's porch, drawing room and sit-out — those spans will need an RCC beam or steel joists sized for the balcony above; confirm with your structural engineer.
  4. Every bedroom is en-suite. Each of the three bedrooms gets its own attached toilet and a small dressing buffer, which the ground floor didn't have.
This is a conceptual space-planning proposal, not a construction drawing. Wall thicknesses, beam sizes, column positions and services (electrical, plumbing, drainage) are not engineered here. Before building, have a licensed structural engineer verify that the ground floor's walls, columns and footing can carry a second storey, and have an architect finalise dimensioned working drawings.
