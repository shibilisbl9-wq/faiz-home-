Design Notes

  1. Same footprint, same stair. Both floors are 1280 × 898 cm and the stair hall sits in the same 110 × 568 cm slot on both sheets.
  2. Four shared walls carry the load. The verticals at 500, 810 and 920 cm and the horizontal at 568 cm run through both plans, so the first floor's main partitions land on ground floor walls.
  3. Balcony over porch. The 300×330 cm balcony sits directly above the ground floor's open porch — same footprint, so it can be carried on the same corner columns rather than a new long-span beam.
  4. Sit-out becomes an enclosed room. The ground floor's sit-out projects beyond the main rectangle and isn't built over; the first floor's Living room instead ends at the main 898 cm wall, sitting on the drawing room's own south wall.
Both plans are conceptual, not construction drawings. The ground floor was redrawn from your photographed 17/1/15 sheet using its labelled room sizes — if any wall, door or window position looks off, tell me and I'll correct it. Wall thicknesses, beam sizes, column positions and services (electrical, plumbing, drainage) are not engineered here. Before building, have a licensed structural engineer verify that the ground floor can carry a second storey, and have an architect finalise dimensioned working drawings.
